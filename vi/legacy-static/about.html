The Company

A plastics division built to serve global customers

GRG Polydynamics Private Limited is a plastics division started in the year 2011 to serve automotive and non-automotive customers.

The company has grown and expanded its operations into precision and high-quality plastic injection moulding components for automotive, electrical and electronics, industrial and machine, and consumer product applications. The company engages product-specific experts to support global customers, meet their requirements and achieve customer delight.

Operations are based at Vallakottai, Sriperumbudur — inside the Chennai automotive manufacturing belt, close to the OEM and tier-one supply base we serve.

How We Operate

The operating model

  • Requirement first. Nothing enters the plant before a customer requirement review and feasibility study is closed.
  • Design for manufacturability. Product design is evaluated for mouldability, tolerance capability and tooling implication before commitment.
  • Qualify the tool. Moulds are manufactured and qualified against a defined acceptance standard, not a trial-and-error loop.
  • Lock the process. Parameters are developed, optimised and captured in a control plan that production runs against.
  • Prove it dimensionally. Sample production is verified against drawing before PPAP submission.
  • Improve continuously. Post-launch performance feeds back into parameters, maintenance intervals and documentation.
