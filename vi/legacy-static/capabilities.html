Capability 01 & 02

Product Development & Prototyping

Programmes start with a requirement review, not a quotation. We evaluate the design against our process capability, flag manufacturability risk while change is still inexpensive, and prove the concept physically before production tooling is committed.

  • Customer requirement review and feasibility study
  • Design for Manufacturability (DFM) analysis
  • Material selection and validation
  • Prototype samples for form, fit and function
  • Dimensional verification against drawing

Capability 03

Mould Making

Tool concept, cavitation and layout are developed and formally approved with the customer before steel is cut. Moulds are then manufactured and qualified against a defined acceptance standard covering fit, function and dimensional output.

  • Tooling concept development
  • Tool design and customer approval
  • Mould manufacturing and qualification
  • Trial validation and optimisation
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling

Capability 05 & 06

Painting, Chrome Plating & Printing

Appearance-critical parts are finished in-house. Keeping decoration on the same site as moulding shortens the loop between a surface defect and the process parameter that caused it — and removes a transport stage from the programme.

  • Painting for decorative and functional finishes
  • Chrome plating on moulded components
  • Screen printing
  • Pad printing for graphics, legends and markings

Capability 08

Tooling Transfer Support

Moving a tool between suppliers or plants is a controlled engineering exercise, not a logistics one. We take responsibility for the transfer, the assessment and the stabilisation of the process that follows.

  • Mould transfer and validation
  • Preventive maintenance (PM)
  • PREP assessment and process stabilisation
  • Production transition support
  • Trial validation and optimisation
Transfer Programme

Transfer

Tool receipt, condition survey and validation against the incoming specification.

PREP Assessment

Structured assessment of tool readiness, wear items and required refurbishment.

Stabilisation

Trial validation and parameter optimisation until output is repeatable and capable.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled PM intervals to protect cycle time, tool life and dimensional stability.
