Who We Are

A plastics division built on group engineering strength

GRG Polydynamics Private Limited is part of a professionally managed group of companies with a global presence, three decades of business endeavours and a turnover above ₹300 crores. The plastics division was started in 2011 to serve automotive and non-automotive customers.

The company has grown and expanded its operations into precision, high-quality plastic injection moulding components for automotive, electrical and electronics, industrial and machine, and consumer product applications — engaging product-specific experts to support global customers and meet their requirements.
