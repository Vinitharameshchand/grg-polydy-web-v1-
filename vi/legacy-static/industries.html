Our domain expertise

Four sectors, each with its own acceptance criteria — served by one quality system and one set of documented process controls.

AutomotiveElectrical & ElectronicsIndustrial & MachineConsumer Products

Sector 01

Automotive

The plastics division was established in 2011 to serve automotive customers, and automotive discipline still sets the standard for everything else we run. Our IATF 16949 certification governs traceability, control plans and change management on every automotive part number.

  • Precision moulded components for automotive assemblies
  • PPAP and APQP documentation as standard deliverables
  • Control-plan driven production with in-process inspection
  • Located inside the Sriperumbudur automotive belt, near the OEM supply base
IATF 16949

Sector 02

Electrical & Electronics

Electrical and electronics components carry safety and insulation requirements that make material selection and process consistency inseparable from the part itself. Both are validated before a tool is released to production.

  • Insulating housings, enclosures and switchgear parts
  • Engineering-grade polymer selection and validation
  • Dimensional stability for assembly and contact fit
  • Traceable material and process records
Engineering Polymers

Sector 03

Industrial & Machine Components

Functional machine components are judged on whether they assemble and keep working. Repeatability across a production run — not a single good sample — is what our process lock-down and preventive maintenance schedule exist to protect.

  • Functional components for industrial machinery
  • Tight dimensional tolerance and stability
  • Custom manufacturing support, including metal casting
  • Low- and high-volume programmes
Precision Production

Sector 04

Consumer Products

Consumer parts are appearance-critical, and appearance defects are process defects. Our in-house painting, chrome plating and pad printing keep decoration on the same site as moulding, so a surface issue is traced back to its cause without shipping parts anywhere.

  • Appearance-critical moulded components
  • In-house painting and chrome plating
  • Screen and pad printing for graphics and legends
  • Cosmetic inspection against agreed acceptance standards
Finished In-House
